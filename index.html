Your visit, in four words

About an hour for a cut, an afternoon for colour. Here's what happens in the chair.

  1. Talk

    We start at the mirror: your hair, your routine, the photo you saved. We agree on the result before anything is mixed.

  2. Colour

    Your shade is mixed for you and checked at every step, from the first foil to the final gloss.

  3. Cut

    Creative cuts shaped to how your hair falls and how you'll actually wear it at home.

  4. Glow

    Blow-dry, finish, and the tips and products to keep it glowing until your next visit.

Good to know

How long does a balayage take?

Usually an afternoon. It depends on your length and the colour you start from; we'll give you a time at the consultation.

Do I need a consultation before colour?

Every colour starts with one at the mirror, on the day. For a big change, come by or send us a photo of your hair beforehand.

Can I bring an inspiration photo?

Please do. Screenshots from Instagram are perfect, including ours.

Do you do bridal hair?

Yes: a trial first, then the day itself. Message us with your date so we can keep it free.
